Why Kushwell Takes Security Seriously

In healthcare, security is often discussed in terms of compliance. At Kushwell, we view security as something deeper: a moral obligation to patients.

HIPAA Is a Baseline — Not a Finish Line

HIPAA was designed to protect patient health information in traditional clinical environments. Cannabis does not operate in that environment.

Cannabis patients interact with dispensaries, budtenders, digital platforms, loyalty systems, payment providers, and regulatory databases — often across jurisdictions with conflicting laws. This fragmentation creates exposure that HIPAA alone was never designed to address.

Treating HIPAA as “good enough” in cannabis is not diligence — it is negligence.

Why Kushwell Exceeds HIPAA

  • Data minimization by design — We collect only what is necessary.
  • Patient-controlled visibility — Patients determine how data is used.
  • No paid access to patient information — Ever.
  • Strict internal access boundaries — Even within Kushwell.
  • Auditability and traceability — Every access has accountability.

Security is not just encryption and firewalls. It is governance, restraint, and the refusal to monetize trust.

The Stakes Are Higher in Cannabis

Cannabis still carries stigma. Employment consequences, insurance risks, and legal ambiguity remain real concerns for many patients.

When platforms treat cannabis data casually, patients bear the risk — not the companies. Kushwell exists to reverse that equation.

Security as a Standard of Care

We believe patient protection should be treated as a standard of care, not a legal obligation to be satisfied at minimum cost.

That belief shapes every architectural, operational, and ethical decision we make. It is not marketing language. It is infrastructure.

Trust is fragile. Kushwell was built to earn it — and to keep it.
