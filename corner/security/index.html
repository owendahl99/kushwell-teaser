Cannabis patients share information that is personal, stigmatized, and often legally sensitive. Yet much of the industry treats data protection as an afterthought — or worse, a marketing checkbox.

Kushwell was designed from day one with the belief that patient trust cannot exist without security that exceeds minimum standards.

While HIPAA sets a baseline for healthcare data protection, it was written for a traditional medical system — not a fragmented cannabis ecosystem involving dispensaries, platforms, partners, and evolving regulations.

Kushwell goes beyond compliance. We design security as a system of restraint, control, and accountability — giving patients clarity over how their data is used, shared, and protected.
