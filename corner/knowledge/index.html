Why research was never prioritized — and why that gap still matters.

Home → The Kushwell Corner → Knowledge

Stigma played a role — but the deeper truth is simpler: cannabis research was never economically prioritized.

Cannabis is one of the most widely used therapeutic plants in the world — and one of the least understood.

For decades, patients have been forced to navigate wellness decisions with fragmented information, anecdotal advice, and retail environments optimized for speed and price rather than understanding.

The result is a persistent knowledge gap — not because people don’t care, but because the systems around them were never designed to support deep, patient-centered education.

Why this matters

Cannabis outcomes vary dramatically by strain, terpene profile, dosage, frequency, and individual physiology. Without accessible, structured knowledge, patients are left guessing — and providers are left without the tools needed to guide responsibly.

Kushwell exists to close this gap — not by overwhelming patients with data, but by organizing knowledge in ways that are practical, respectful, and grounded in real-world experience.
